Privacy Policy for The Crate Escape

Effective Date: 8 March 2026.

Welcome to The Crate Escape! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a player.

What We Collect

Google account data collection is optional and only applies if you choose Google Sign-In for cloud sync.

When you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive the following information:

  • Your email address
  • Your Google display name

This data is stored securely in a Google Firestore database when cloud sync is enabled. Parts of this data (such as display name or email) may be shown to you in cloud sync/account status screens inside the game.

Why We Store It

The purposes below apply only when you choose optional cloud sign-in:

We use this data solely to:

  • Save your game progress
  • Sync your progress across devices you use to play The Crate Escape
  • Identify your account if you request data deletion or support

We do not use your personal data for marketing, profiling, or in-game personalisation.

Analytics

We use a privacy-focused analytics tool to measure aggregate website traffic, such as page views, visit counts, and high-level browser/device statistics.

GoatCounter is an open-source, privacy-friendly analytics service. It records page views and basic visit data (such as browser type and country) without using cookies or tracking individuals across sites. GoatCounter's privacy policy is available at goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

We do not use analytics to track individual gameplay events (for example levels completed, moves, or puzzle actions).

Authentication

The Crate Escape offers optional Google Sign-In for cloud sync authentication. You can play without signing in. If you choose to sign in, we do not collect or store passwords. Your login credentials are managed securely by Google.

What We Don’t Do

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data
  • Display your personal info publicly to other players
  • Track your activity outside of The Crate Escape
  • Use analytics to profile you through individual gameplay actions

We do use trusted service providers such as Google/Firebase (for optional sign-in and cloud save) and GoatCounter (for open-source, cookie-free page view analytics).

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Just email us at unimatrix@korovatron.co.uk, and we’ll verify your identity using your Google account details before proceeding.

Security

If you choose optional Google Sign-In/cloud sync, your account and cloud-save data are stored in Google Firestore, a secure cloud-hosted NoSQL database. We follow best practices to protect your information, but no system is 100% foolproof. We rely on Google’s robust infrastructure and encourage you to sign out when you’re done playing.
